# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the GitAR project a respectful, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- communicating respectfully
- giving and receiving constructive technical feedback
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than individuals
- respecting differing levels of experience
- helping keep discussions relevant and productive
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them when appropriate

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory or degrading comments
- personal attacks
- deliberate disruption of project discussions
- publication of another person's private information without permission
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer, **Reza Moussavi**, through the contact options available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to GitAR project spaces and to public interactions representing the project.
